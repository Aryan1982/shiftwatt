Fair questions.

Do I need to buy anything?

No. It works through the apps your charger and appliances already have.

Is the grid data real?

Yes. Great Britain is live from NESO, including the landscape at the top of this page.

What never gets shifted?

Fridges, freezers, medical equipment. Only what you mark as flexible.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Clean hours are often cheaper hours, too.
